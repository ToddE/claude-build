# inform9 Functional Requirements

## Document Notes

- 355 requirements from 30 use cases. Source use cases: [use-cases.md](use-cases.md).
- Priority uses MoSCoW by this rule: Must for Basic Path behavior, security, data integrity, abuse controls, and consent. Should for other alternate paths and error handling. Could for convenience paths. Count: Must 256, Should 95, Could 4. Review these before import.
- Component names follow the use case actors: inform9 Platform, Email Service, and Payment Processor.
- Each requirement cites the Basic Path step, Alternate or Exception Path label, or Post-Condition it came from. Acceptance Criteria reuse the Post-Condition wording where one applies.
- This file is the only place requirements are edited. A Jira-import CSV can be generated from it with `python3 tools/requirements_md_to_csv.py`. The CSV is not kept.

## Gaps to resolve

These items appear in the use cases as open or as placeholders. No requirement was written for them without a decision.

- Password rules and multi-factor sign-in for Business Owner and Payee accounts (Create Account and Sign In, Create Payee Account).
- Required fields for a business (Add Business, Edit Business).
- Request link lifetime [REQUEST_LINK_DAYS], at least 30 days with the default reminders (Request W-9, Send W-9 Reminders).
- Which signature details to record, such as time and IP address, and whether a Payee can save partial progress (Complete and Sign W-9).
- IRS conditions for electronic signature, certification, and one-click approval. Legal review before launch (Complete and Sign W-9, Confirm and Reuse Saved Payee Information, Update a W-9).
- Protections for stored taxpayer IDs, such as encryption, access control, and audit logging (Complete and Sign W-9, Export Payee Data).
- Retention and deletion policy [RETENTION_POLICY], with legal review of IRS record-keeping periods (Cancel Account and Data Handling).
- Pricing tiers, renewal and lapse rules, refunds, sales tax, and [PAID_LIMIT] (Upgrade After Free Limit).
- Whether the Payee enters the recipient email twice, and values for [DAILY_SEND_CAP] and [REPEAT_WINDOW] (Payee Sends W-9 to Business, Limit Unsolicited Sends).
- Detection rules for abusive sending, and how the administrator reviews and lifts a pause (Limit Unsolicited Sends).
- One-time code length, lifetime, and attempt limits: [CODE_MINUTES], [MAX_CODE_ATTEMPTS], [LOCK_MINUTES] (Business Recipient Retrieves Payee-Sent W-9, Opt Out of Payee-Sent W-9s).
- Follow-up trigger [FOLLOW_UP_DAYS] (Follow Up on Incomplete Request).
- Whether downloads by a Business Owner are logged for audit (View and Download W-9s). The use case lists this as open, so no requirement exists yet.
- A Consent record for update emails: when each person agreed and the wording they saw. The use case lists this as open, so no requirement exists yet (Manage Communication Preferences).
- Environment variables as the first-release source for link expiry and the daily cap, with the administrator screen as the target (Configure Platform Settings).
- Export column mappings for QuickBooks Online and Xero, checked against the current official import templates (Export Payee Data).
- Values for [MAX_RESENDS], [PAID_LIMIT], [PAID_BUSINESS_LIMIT], [REPORT_THRESHOLD], [REVIEW_DAYS], and [IDLE_MINUTES] (Follow Up on Incomplete Request, Upgrade After Free Limit, Review Abuse Flags, Sign Out).
- What happens to a person's saved W-9 information and Payee sign-in after the Business Owner role is canceled, since one account holds both roles (Cancel Account and Data Handling). No requirement was written for this.
- Marketing email rules (for example CAN-SPAM) for reminder, payee-sent, and update emails. Legal review before launch.
