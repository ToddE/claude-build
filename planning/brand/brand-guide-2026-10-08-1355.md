# inform9 Brand Guide

## 1. Idea

inform9 does one job: it collects a W-9 and files it. The brand should feel calm, exact, and trustworthy, like a well-kept ledger, with one warm accent for the next action. It avoids the look of a generic software template: no gradients, no glossy cards, no stock illustrations, no purple-to-pink washes.

## 2. Logo

The wordmark is set in lowercase, drawn from round-ended strokes of one weight. The "9" is a blue ring with a green stem. It sits at ascender height and ends on the baseline, so it reads as a numeral. It carries the W-9 reference and works alone as the mark.

Rules:
- Keep clear space on every side equal to the width of the "9" ring.
- Smallest sizes: wordmark 80 px wide, mark 16 px.
- Do not stretch, outline, add shadows, or recolor. Use only the light and reverse versions.
- The letters are drawn as shapes, so the files do not depend on a font.

### The pointing 9

The ring and stem of the 9 can read as a fist with a pointing finger. The brand uses this only as a quiet motif and never draws a hand.
- **Motion:** On hover or press of the main button, the green stem of a 9 shown beside it, or the mark in a loading state, moves down 2 px for 150 ms, like pressing a key.
- **Empty states:** The mark sits next to the one action to take, such as "Add your first payee".
- **Copy:** "One press to request. One press to approve."
- The mark is not recolored orange, and it is not enlarged into an illustration.
- The logo shape stays as drawn. The wordmark letter spacing was set on 2026-10-08 and approved.

Use rules:
- Orange is only for the one main action on a screen, such as Send request, Sign and submit, or Upgrade. Use it once per screen.
- Blue is for everything else that is clickable. Green marks completion and success, never a button label.
- Orange button text is ink, not white. White on this orange fails contrast.
- Dark mode lifts blue to #8FA8FF and green to #3DCB91 so both stay readable on ink.
- Color is never the only signal. Every status chip has a word.

Status chips (request and share statuses):

## 4. Type

| Use | Font | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headings | Source Serif 4, semibold | Gives a ledger-like, established feel |
| Interface and body | IBM Plex Sans | Clear numerals for taxpayer IDs and dates |
| Codes and IDs | IBM Plex Mono | One-time codes, request numbers |

- Self-host the fonts from the Cloudflare site to avoid a third-party request on pages that handle tax data.
- Body 16 px with 1.6 line height. Sign-in, form labels, and buttons 16 px or larger. Headings use a 1.25 scale.
- Use tabular numerals in tables and for dates.

## 5. Shape, space, and motion

- 4 px spacing base. Radius 10 px on cards and inputs, 6 px on chips and small controls, 16 px on large panels.
- Flat surfaces with a 1 px border. A single soft shadow only for menus and dialogs.
- Icons are outline icons with a 1.75 px stroke and round ends, to match the logo strokes.
- Motion is short (150 ms) and only confirms an action. Respect reduced-motion settings.

## 6. Components (starting rules)

- **Primary button:** orange fill, ink text, 44 px tall minimum, one per screen.
- **Secondary button:** blue outline, blue text.
- **Link:** blue, underlined.
- **Form field:** visible label above the input, 44 px tall, 1 px border, blue focus ring 2 px with 2 px offset, error text in Delivery failed red with an icon.
- **Signature box:** large, bordered, with a baseline rule and a Clear button. Works with touch.
- **Taxpayer ID field:** shows the last four digits after entry, never the full number on screen again.
- **Status chip:** word plus color, as in the table above.

## 7. Voice

- Plain and direct. Short sentences. No jargon without a definition.
- Address the person: "Send the request", "Your W-9 is saved".
- Say what happens next on every screen that asks for something.
- Do not use exclamation marks, hype, or blame in error messages.

## 8. Accessibility

- Contrast: text 4.5:1 or better, large text and controls 3:1 or better. The pairs above meet this.
- Every control works with a keyboard, with a visible focus ring.
- Tap targets are 44 px or larger, since many payees will use a phone.
- Light and dark themes both ship in the first release.
